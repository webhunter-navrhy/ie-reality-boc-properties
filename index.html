Description

The Haggard, Annaholty, Birdhill, Co. Tipperary, V94 E1V7

BOC Properties are delighted to present this superb four-bedroom dormer-style home, newly introduced to the market. Located in one of the most sought-after areas on the outskirts of Birdhill, this property offers an excellent opportunity for prospective purchasers.

The property is presented in turnkey condition and is ready for immediate occupation.

At the heart of the home is a bright, open-plan kitchen, living and dining area, featuring an impressive vaulted ceiling and a wood-burning stove as its centrepiece. This wonderful space flows seamlessly onto a sun-soaked patio area, perfect for outdoor entertaining and relaxation.

The ground floor also benefits from two spacious bedrooms, a main bathroom and a utility room.

Upstairs offers a further two generous bedrooms, both with built-in wardrobes, along with a second bathroom.

The residence boasts stylish décor throughout and is finished to a high standard with quality fixtures and fittings. This is a home that is ready to move into and enjoy, with no additional expenditure required.

Set on beautifully landscaped gardens and complete with a large detached garage, the property enjoys a prime position on a quiet road, offering both privacy and convenience while remaining within easy reach of Limerick City.

Viewings will commence from 22nd September. For further information or to arrange a viewing, please contact Brendan or Joe at BOC Properties.
